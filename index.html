TTiming plan

Week by week, from now to the CRM go-live in early December.

How I've worked this out: I have about 60 hours a month for Thompsons For Travel, alongside 90 hours for Grosvenor and Edusport. That's about 15 hours a week here: roughly 3 for ongoing support (Tommy checks, questions from the team, the monthly report) and 12 for this plan. The hours are my rough estimates. Chat work is marked with a cyan bar, about 41h of the 120h before go-live.
Chat comes first. Chat is the main focus, so all the chat work now happens before the CRM go-live instead of in January: the label audit, the anonymous survey, full chat training, chat reporting and the second-response SLA, and the chat times review. To make room, I have:
  • Kept the SharePoint refresh to a brighter theme and brighter photos, rather than a full redesign.
  • Moved the destination, location and newsletter data, and the document review cycle, to January.
  • Brought the confirmation and quote templates back in for go-live, as independents need them. The design brief going out now, with designs back by 30 October, makes room.
  • Folded the Customer Touchpoints update into the template wording, as they cover the same ground.
  • Moved the Ambitions Travel Training courses and the CRM Playbook update to January.
SharePoint parts added for launch: Passport Pro on the Quizzes page, the Training Calendar on the Home page, and a placeholder for educationals. They fit into October by moving the items above.
